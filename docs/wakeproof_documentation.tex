\begin{wpbox}[wpnavy]{Executive Summary}
Most productivity tools fail at the exact moment willpower is lowest: at \textbf{05:30} when a sleepy thumb hits \emph{Snooze}, mid-lecture when a 25-minute timer offers a one-tap \emph{Stop} button, or at night when ``just five minutes'' of short-form video becomes two hours.

\textbf{WakeProof} eliminates negotiation across the entire daily cycle while remaining \textbf{private and offline-first by design}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Physical-Proof Morning Alarms:} Alarms carry no dismiss button. Ringing stops only after live on-device ML~Kit pose detection counts your push-ups, on-device computer vision verifies water left your bottle, or you solve an unbroken run of mental arithmetic.
  \item \textbf{Nuclear Deep-Work Sessions:} Fixed-duration Pomodoro recipes (1\,h, 1\,h\,30, 2\,h, 3\,h, or exact calendar-event durations) have \textbf{no pause and no stop button}, walling non-study apps across your phone, tablet, and smartwatch simultaneously --- breaks included.
  \item \textbf{Integrated Study Suite:} Spaced-repetition flashcards (SM-2) with optional Gemini AI card drafting, circular stereo soundscapes, Spotify transport control, Focus Forest tree-guarding, exam countdowns, parked thoughts, and an on-device weekly coach report.
  \item \textbf{Hardened Distraction Schedule \& Minimalist Launcher:} Social, video, messaging, portal, and browser apps open \textbf{only Monday--Thursday from 08:10 to 08:50} and stay \textbf{completely shut Friday--Sunday}, backed by a 7-day cooling-off lock on rule changes, notification/media guards, a night sleep wall (19:45--03:00), and a calm text-only home screen.
\end{enumerate}
\end{wpbox}

\subsection{Single-Device Alarm Ringing Role}
When Cloud Sync links a phone and a tablet, alarm rows synchronize so both screens show accurate schedules and daily goals. However, \textbf{ringing} is governed locally in device-protected storage (\texttt{AlarmDevicePolicy}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item Devices with telephony default to \textbf{Ringing ON}; Wi-Fi tablets default to \textbf{Ringing OFF} and display a clear companion banner on the Home dashboard.
  \item Turning ringing off on a companion device actively cancels all local \texttt{AlarmManager} intents via \texttt{AlarmScheduler}, guaranteeing zero ghost rings even before first unlock after a reboot.
\end{itemize}

\section{Camera- \& Mission-Verified Morning Alarms}

\subsection{High-Reliability Alarm Engine}
WakeProof schedules alarms with \texttt{AlarmManager.setAlarmClock()}, granting OS-level Doze immunity, status-bar alarm-clock visibility, and exact firing.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Direct-Boot Mirror:} Every armed alarm's time and weekday bitmask (\texttt{hour:minute:mask}) are mirrored into Android \emph{Device-Protected Storage} (\texttt{DirectBootAlarmStore}). If the phone reboots overnight and sits at the PIN screen before first unlock, WakeProof still wakes the screen and rings on time.
  \item \textbf{Weekly Repeat Bitmasks:} Each alarm carries a 7-bit mask (\texttt{Monday = bit 0 \dots Sunday = bit 6}). Empty masks are rejected in the UI and treated as every-day in the scheduler so an alarm can never silently disarm itself.
  \item \textbf{Ghost-Alarm Immunity:} \texttt{AlarmInitializer} diffs Room rows (including deleted tombstones) against \texttt{DirectBootAlarmStore} IDs on every startup and sync pass, cancelling daily, snooze (\texttt{+20,000}), and test (\texttt{+10,000}) PendingIntents together.
  \item \textbf{Clock-Format Respect:} All pickers and labels automatically honour the user's system 12-hour or 24-hour clock setting (\texttt{AlarmTimeFormat}) so AM/PM schedules are never mis-entered.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Three Verification Missions}

\subsubsection[1. Push-Up Verification (100\% On-Device ML Kit)]{1. Push-Up Verification \texorpdfstring{\pill{100\% On-Device ML Kit}}{(100\% On-Device ML Kit)}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{How it works:} CameraX streams frames (front or rear camera, flippable live) into Google ML~Kit Pose Detection on the device. A pure state machine (\texttt{PushupCounter}) tracks shoulder--elbow--wrist joint angles across down and up phases, displaying a live pose skeleton and rep counter.
  \item \textbf{Target range:} Configurable from \textbf{5 to 10 push-ups} (\texttt{MAX\_PUSHUP\_TARGET = 10}), keeping mornings sharp and sustainable without joint strain.
  \item \textbf{Progressive Ringtone Ducking:} Volume ramps in smoothly until rep~1 is registered, then steps down progressively from $60\%$ to $15\%$ with each completed rep (\texttt{pushupRingVolume}) so the alarm stops shouting while you are actively exercising --- snapping back to full volume if you stop.
  \item \textbf{Zero retention:} Camera frames are analysed in memory and immediately discarded; no photo or video is ever saved to disk or sent over the network.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection[2. Water-Drinking Verification (On-Device CV + Optional Gemini Second Opinion)]{2. Water-Drinking Verification \texorpdfstring{\pill{On-Device CV + Optional Gemini Second Opinion}}{(On-Device CV + Optional Gemini Second Opinion)}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Two-photo protocol:} Photograph your translucent water bottle (\emph{Before}), drink, and photograph it again (\emph{After}). Capture runs at low-latency $\sim 1280\times 960$ resolution with automatic EXIF orientation normalization (\texttt{WaterImageSampler}).
  \item \textbf{Geometry-first on-device detector (\texttt{WaterLineDetector}):} Rather than looking for the biggest brightness step (which is easily fooled by background clutter seen through clear plastic), WakeProof measures \textbf{across-column horizontal coherence of the vertical gradient}, gated by a two-region Welch $t$-statistic and damped where paired opposite edges indicate a printed bottle label rather than a liquid meniscus.
  \item \textbf{Same-bottle verification (\texttt{ContainerMatcher}):} Compares lighting-invariant chromaticity histograms only across the bands unchanged by drinking (air above the higher water line and water below the lower line), remaining robust when indoor lighting shifts.
  \item \textbf{Verdict \& multi-sip chaining (\texttt{WaterVerdictRules}):} Consumed volume is $\lfloor \Delta\text{fill} \times \text{bottleCapacityMl} \rfloor_{10\,\text{ml}}$ (bottle size configurable $250\text{--}2000\,\text{ml}$, default $750\,\text{ml}$). A sip is accepted when it is the same container, readable in both shots, not refilled, and shows $\ge 30\,\text{ml}$ and $\ge 5\%$ drop. You do not have to chug in one go: each accepted \emph{After} photo automatically chains into the next \emph{Before} photo until cumulative intake reaches target (within a $50\,\text{ml}$ tolerance).
  \item \textbf{Cloud second opinion:} On-device acceptances finish instantly with zero network traffic. Only if the on-device detector rejects a pair \emph{and} you have configured a Gemini API key does \texttt{LocalFirstWaterObserver} request a second opinion from \texttt{gemini-3.8-flash} (capped at an $8\,\text{s}$ timeout) before surfacing a rejection.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection[3. Mental Math Verification (Camera-Free / Dark-Room Ready)]{3. Mental Math Verification \texorpdfstring{\pill{Camera-Free / Dark-Room Ready}}{(Camera-Free / Dark-Room Ready)}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Designed for dark rooms, shared bedrooms, or mornings where camera missions are impractical.
  \item Requires solving \textbf{1 to 10 consecutive arithmetic problems} (default 3) with positive $\le 4$-digit answers.
  \item \textbf{Anti-guessing rule:} Entering a single wrong answer resets your current streak back to \texttt{0} \emph{and} generates a brand-new problem, making digit-by-digit brute forcing impossible. On tablets in landscape, the keypad splits into an ergonomic two-pane problem/keypad layout.
\end{itemize}

\section{Nuclear Focus, Soundscapes \& Focus Forest}

\subsection{Unstoppable Pomodoro Sessions (\texttt{PomodoroTimerService})}
Standard timers fail because quitting takes a single tap the moment boredom hits. WakeProof implements \textbf{Nuclear Focus}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fixed Commitments Only:} Open-ended ``free-run'' timers are intentionally absent because an unstoppable session must have a guaranteed end time. Choose from \textbf{1\,h}, \textbf{1\,h\,30}, \textbf{2\,h}, or the fixed \textbf{3\,h recipe} ($6 \times 25\,\text{min}$ focus blocks, $5\,\text{min}$ short breaks, and a $15\,\text{min}$ long break after the third block $= 3\,\text{h}\,05\,\text{m}$), or launch an \textbf{exact-duration session from a calendar event}.
  \item \textbf{Zero Pause, Reset, or Stop:} Once started, pause and stop controls disappear from the app UI, notification shade, floating overlay bubble, and watch remote. Monotonic timing (\texttt{SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()}) prevents tick drift.
  \item \textbf{Whole-Session Cross-Device Focus Wall:} From the first second until the final block ends --- \textbf{including breaks}, so a 5-minute break never turns into an hour of scrolling --- every app outside your pre-approved \emph{Study Apps} list is walled across your phone, your tablet (\texttt{LiveFocusSession}), and your watch (\texttt{WatchSessionBeacon}).
  \item \textbf{Mid-Session Tamper Locks:} During an active session, Android \texttt{Settings} is walled (\texttt{FOCUS\_WALLED\_ESSENTIALS}), study apps can be \emph{removed} from the focus list but never \emph{added}, and Priority Do Not Disturb (\texttt{SessionDnd}) engages automatically across every walled device.
  \item \textbf{Reboot \& Force-Stop Persistence (\texttt{SessionRecovery}):} Session state commits to disk on every phase transition. Rebooting or killing the process resumes the session, marks any interrupted focus block as \emph{Withered}, and logs overdue blocks as \texttt{SKIPPED}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Full-Screen Immersive Mode, Floating Bubble \& Thought Parking}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Full-Screen Immersive Timer:} During focus phases, WakeProof hides navigation drawers, chat panels, sound pickers, and subject chips, leaving only the progress ring, wall-clock break time (\emph{``Break at 14:35''}), and \textbf{Park a thought}.
  \item \textbf{Thought Parking:} Capture intrusive reminders or to-dos in two seconds mid-block without opening a browser or messaging app; parked thoughts sync across devices for review after the session.
  \item \textbf{Floating Bubble \& Keep-Screen-On:} When switching into an approved study app (such as Samsung Notes), a non-focusable overlay bubble keeps the countdown visible on top and optionally holds \texttt{FLAG\_KEEP\_SCREEN\_ON} across the entire session.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Circular Stereo Soundscapes \& Spotify Control}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{7 Synthesized Circular Stereo Loops:} Rain, Fireplace, Ocean Waves, Forest Creek, Warm Ambient Pad, Soft Pink Static, and Wind in the Pines --- encoded as $44.1\,\text{kHz}$ stereo OGG Vorbis loops (\texttt{tools/sounds/}). Every loop is constructed circularly in the frequency domain (zero crossfade dip) and levelled to equal K-weighted loudness, with configurable \textbf{0--60\,s squared-curve fade-in and pre-rolled fade-out}.
  \item \textbf{SDK-Free Spotify Integration (\texttt{SpotifyController}):} Save any playlist URI in Settings, launch it directly, and control play/pause/next/previous via Android media key dispatch --- requiring zero Spotify account login or SDK telemetry. Selecting Spotify as your focus sound automatically whitelists Spotify on the focus wall for that session.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Focus Forest \& Focus-First Streaks}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Focus Forest (\texttt{FocusTree}):} Every verified focus block plants a tree in your visual forest (derived directly from \texttt{completion\_logs}, so your forest syncs and backs up automatically). With \emph{Deep Focus} active, spending more than $10\,\text{s}$ in any non-study, non-essential app withers the current block's tree (\texttt{SKIPPED}). Turning the screen off never penalizes you.
  \item \textbf{Focus-First Daily Streak (\texttt{DailyGoals}):} Your headline daily streak increments whenever verified focus minutes reach your \textbf{Daily Focus Goal} (configurable up to \textbf{12 hours} in 15-minute steps and synced across devices so phone and tablet always report the exact same streak).
\end{itemize}

\section{Study Suite: SM-2 Flashcards, Exams, Analytics \& Coach}

\subsection{SM-2 Spaced-Repetition Flashcards \& Gemini Drafting}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Classic SM-2 Algorithm (\texttt{Sm2Scheduler}):} Cards track ease factor, repetition count, and next-review epoch day across \emph{Again}, \emph{Hard}, \emph{Good}, and \emph{Easy} ratings, organized by subject tag.
  \item \textbf{AI Flashcard Drafting (Optional):} Paste lecture notes or snap/pick a photo of handwritten or printed pages to draft review-ready Q\&A cards via Google's \texttt{gemini-3.8-flash} model using strict JSON schema enforcement (\texttt{FlashcardEnvelope}).
  \item \textbf{In-Timer Study Coach Chat (Optional):} Ask conceptual questions during study breaks using your own Gemini API key (\texttt{reasoning\_effort = "low"} keeps latency crisp).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Exams, Cross-Device Study Time \& On-Device Weekly Coach}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Exam Countdowns:} Track upcoming exams with subject tags and days-remaining badges directly on the Home dashboard.
  \item \textbf{Cross-Device Study-Time Reconciliation (\texttt{StudyTime}):} Studying on a tablet, on paper, or with the watch timer leaves zero foreground events on your phone. WakeProof publishes each device's daily study-app usage (\texttt{studyUsage/\{installId\}}) and credits any unmeasured timer shortfall ($\max(\text{measured}, \text{focus})$) to your primary study app (default: Samsung Notes) --- capturing paper and wrist sessions without ever double-counting screen study.
  \item \textbf{Raw Event-Folded Screen Time (\texttt{UsageStats\allowbreak Repository}):} Android's built-in \texttt{queryAnd\allowbreak Aggregate\allowbreak UsageStats} leaks pre-midnight hours into ``today'' and misses active foreground sessions. WakeProof instead folds raw \texttt{UsageEvents} per activity class (\texttt{foldForeground\allowbreak Millis}, \texttt{foregroundMillis\allowbreak PerSpan}), splitting cross-midnight sessions cleanly at \texttt{00:00} and accurately tracking multi-activity trampolining apps.
  \item \textbf{100\% On-Device Weekly Report (\texttt{WeeklyReportGenerator}):} Synthesizes your past 7 days of focus minutes, alarm completions, distraction minutes, and wall stats into \emph{Wins}, up to three severity-ranked \emph{Watch-outs}, and \emph{one concrete suggestion for next week} --- completely offline, with zero data sent to any AI provider.
\end{itemize}

\section{Distraction Walls, Schedule Rules \& Device Hardening}

\subsection{Always-On Gatekeeper \& Verdict Priority}
\texttt{AppBlockService} runs as a lightweight \texttt{specialUse} foreground service (never abusing Android's \texttt{AccessibilityService}), evaluating the live foreground activity set every \textbf{500\,ms} while the screen is interactive and waking immediately on unlock (\texttt{ACTION\_USER\_PRESENT}). Every tick evaluates a single pure function (\texttt{gateVerdict}) in strict priority order:

\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}c L{3.4cm} Y@{}}
\toprule

\subsection{7-Day Cooling-Off, Notification/Media Guards \& Device Owner Mode}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Asymmetric 7-Day Cooling-Off (\texttt{168 hours}):} Tightening any daily app limit or wall rule takes effect immediately. Loosening a cap, widening the open window, or disabling weekend shutdown queues for \textbf{7 full days} and promotes \emph{only} while automatic network time (\texttt{AUTO\_TIME}) is active.
  \item \textbf{Shade \& Media Silencing (\texttt{WindowNotificationGuard}):} Automatically cancels notifications posted by walled apps (inspecting only package names, never message text) and pauses active \texttt{MediaSession} playback (blocking Picture-in-Picture or background audio left running when a window closes).
  \item \textbf{Evening Sleep Hygiene (\texttt{NightRules}):} Delivers a wind-down heads-up at \textbf{19:15}, raises the phone-wide sleep wall at \textbf{19:45}, posts a lights-out cue at \textbf{20:00}, and reminds you every $30\,\text{min}$ if battery is below $40\%$ and unplugged so a dead battery cannot defeat the 03:00 alarm.
  \item \textbf{Optional Sideloaded Device Owner Hardening (\texttt{DeviceLock}):} On personal sideloaded builds provisioned via \texttt{dpm set-device-owner}, WakeProof holds walled apps OS-suspended (\texttt{setPackagesSuspended}, eliminating Samsung pop-up view, split-screen, and Edge-panel workarounds), quarantines newly installed apps for \textbf{7 days} (\texttt{NewAppQuarantine}), enforces a \textbf{90-day cooling-off} before uninstall lock can be released, and blocks Safe Mode, manual clock tampering, secondary user/clone profiles, and default-launcher switching.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Dual-Clock Tombstone Cloud Sync \& Local Backups}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Firestore Mirror (\texttt{SyncEngine}):} Opt-in via Google Credential Manager sign-in (or one-tap phone-to-watch token handoff). Every Room v5 row carries \texttt{syncId}, \texttt{updatedAt}, \texttt{deleted} (tombstone), and \texttt{pending}. Device timestamps arbitrate per-row last-write-wins merges (\texttt{MergeDecision}), while Firestore \texttt{serverTimestamp} drives incremental pull cursors so a skewed device clock can never skip records.
  \item \textbf{Portable Backups:} Export completion history to \textbf{CSV} for spreadsheets, or write/restore a full \textbf{JSON backup} (\texttt{data/backup/}) that merges entities non-destructively (preserving tombstones) and immediately reconciles \texttt{AlarmManager}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{WakeProof Clip --- End-to-End Encrypted Cross-Device Clipboard}
\textbf{WakeProof Clip} unifies the clipboard across your Android phone, Android tablet, and Ubuntu GNOME Wayland laptop for plain text ($\le 256\,\text{KiB}$) and images/screenshots ($\le 20\,\text{MiB}$ on LAN):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Zero-Knowledge Cryptography:} Every payload is sealed with \textbf{AES-256-GCM} using a 32-byte shared key $K$ exchanged strictly over local QR + LAN pairing (TCP port \texttt{47322}, stored in Android \texttt{EncryptedSharedPreferences} and Linux \texttt{libsecret} / GNOME Keyring). Additional authenticated data (\texttt{wakeproof-clip-v1|<uuid>}) prevents replay or tampering.
  \item \textbf{LAN-First, Cloud-Fallback Transport:} Tries direct parallel HTTP delivery over \texttt{\_wakeproofclip\allowbreak .\_tcp} (port \texttt{47321}, $1.5\,\text{s}$ timeout) first; if campus Wi-Fi isolates clients or a peer is remote, falls back to chunked encrypted Firestore documents (\texttt{users/\{uid\}/clips/}) with automatic 1-hour expiry housekeeping. Password-manager clips marked sensitive (\texttt{EXTRA\_IS\_\allowbreak SENSITIVE} / \texttt{x-kde-password\allowbreak ManagerHint}) are never transmitted.
\end{itemize}
